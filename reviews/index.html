How we review

UK Sew reviews are editorial overviews written from published manufacturer information and trade knowledge. We do not invent star ratings, test scores or customer reviews, and no brand pays to appear here. Models change often, so always check the current specification with the manufacturer or a dealer.

The brand matters less than support

Any good machine will need servicing and parts eventually. Choose a model your local dealer can support. In Leicester, PK Sewing Machines lists Singer, Brother, Janome, Juki, Jack, Bernina and Pfaff among the makes it works with. See our PKSM profile.

Want a second opinion?

Independent dealers can tell you which models come back for repair and which keep running. Ask before you buy, and test-sew with your own fabric.

What we look at

  • Build: frame, housing and feel at speed.
  • Feed and stitch quality on light and heavy fabrics.
  • Ease of use: threading, bobbin system, buttonholes.
  • Parts and servicing: can a UK dealer support it for years?
  • Value: features for the money, new or used.

Frequently asked questions

Which sewing machine brand is best?
There is no single best brand. Janome and Brother are popular for home use, Bernina for premium domestic machines, and Juki and Jack for industrial work. Choose a machine your local dealer can service.
Are UK Sew reviews sponsored?
No. Brands do not pay to be reviewed. Some links on Shmor sites may be affiliate links, which are always disclosed.
