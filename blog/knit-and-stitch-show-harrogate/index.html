Knit + Stitch Harrogate 2026

After its October run at Alexandra Palace, Knit + Stitch heads to Harrogate in November. Here is what to know.

By the UK SEW editorial team · 13 October 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: Karly Santiago on Unsplash

Dates

Listings give 19 to 22 November 2026 for the Harrogate show. The London show at Alexandra Palace ran from 8 to 11 October 2026. The show was formerly called The Knitting & Stitching Show. Check the official website for times, tickets and the venue before you travel.

What to expect

Exhibitors selling yarn, fabric, haberdashery and tools, textile art galleries and workshops. It is a good place to see and feel yarns before you buy.

Buying tips

  • Make a list and a budget before you go.
  • Take photos of ball bands and stall details so you can reorder later.
  • For sewing machines, compare show offers with a local dealer who can service the machine later.

What to bring

  • A shopping list with quantities and yarn weights.
  • A tape measure and your pattern measurements.
  • A bag that will take a few kilos of yarn.
  • Comfortable shoes: shows are big.

Workshops and talks

Shows usually run workshops in knitting, crochet, embroidery and textile art, plus talks from makers. Popular sessions sell out, so book early if they need tickets.

Buying a sewing machine at a show

Show offers can be tempting. Before you buy, check who will service the machine and supply parts after the show, the warranty terms, and whether you can return it. A local dealer may match or beat the deal with ongoing support.

Planning your day

  • Check the floor plan online and mark must-see stalls.
  • Arrive early for the quietest browsing.
  • Take breaks: there is usually seating and catering.
  • Keep a note of exhibitors’ websites for later orders.

Other UK craft events

Alongside Knit + Stitch, the UK has many craft shows, yarn festivals and sewing events through the year, from large exhibitions to local wool fairs. Local guilds and sewing groups often organise coach trips.

Closer to home

If you are in the Midlands, PK Sewing Machines in Leicester sells machines, parts and accessories all year. Discover Leicester’s hosiery heritage too, and see our deals page.
