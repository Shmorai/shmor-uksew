Roots in sewing machines

Brother traces its roots to a sewing machine repair business founded in Nagoya, Japan, in 1908. The company grew into a manufacturer of sewing machines and later of printers and many other products.

Domestic machines

Brother’s domestic range runs from compact mechanical machines to computerised models with many stitches, automatic needle threaders and one-step buttonholes. They are a common first machine in UK homes.

Embroidery machines

Brother is well known for embroidery and combination sewing-and-embroidery machines. Check the maximum hoop size and the design file formats each model accepts.

Industrial machines

Brother also makes industrial sewing machines used in workrooms and factories, alongside its domestic range.

Parts and servicing for Brother machines
