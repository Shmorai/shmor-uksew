Leicester · since 1589

The city that clothed the world

For three centuries Leicester’s framework knitters and factories made stockings, socks, underwear and knitwear for Britain and beyond. Corah’s St Margaret’s Works and the Wolsey brand made the city a byword for knitted goods.

That heritage lives on in the city’s knitters, garment makers, dyers and machine engineers.

Local expertise from Leicester

Good advice comes from people who mend machines for a living. Our guides lean on Leicester’s workshop tradition, including PK Sewing Machines on St Saviours Road, a family business since 1982 that sells, services and repairs domestic and industrial machines and keeps a large parts department.

Leicester: the city that clothed the world

Framework knitting reached Leicestershire in the 1600s, and by the 1800s Leicester was famous for stockings, underwear and knitwear. Firms such as N. Corah & Sons, whose St Margaret’s Works was one of the largest hosiery factories in Britain, and Wolsey, named after Cardinal Wolsey who is buried at Leicester Abbey, made the city a byword for knitted goods. Explore Leicester’s hosiery heritage and our UK hosiery sector reading list.

Hosiery, knitwear and the machines behind them

Hosiery means knitted legwear and underwear: socks, tights and stockings. Today’s socks are knitted on circular machines and finished on linking and toe-closing machines, while garments are sewn on overlockers and coverstitch machines, the same types that keep Leicester’s workrooms busy. Read our hosiery and knitwear guide, what denier means and how socks are made.
