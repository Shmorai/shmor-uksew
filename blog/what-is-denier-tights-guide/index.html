What is denier? Tights and hosiery explained

10, 40, 100: the numbers on a pack of tights tell you how sheer or opaque they are. Here is what denier means, and how to choose.

Denier in one sentence

Denier is a unit of fibre weight: the mass in grams of 9,000 metres of a yarn. A finer yarn has a lower denier, so tights knitted from it are sheerer.

Sheer to opaque

DenierLookBest for
Up to about 15Ultra-sheerFormal wear, summer events
About 15–20SheerEveryday office wear
About 30–40Semi-opaqueAutumn, more durability
40 and aboveOpaqueWinter warmth and colour
100+Very opaque, thickCold weather

Brands describe their ranges slightly differently, so check the pack.

Fibres used

  • Nylon (polyamide): the backbone of most tights, strong and smooth.
  • Elastane: gives stretch and recovery for a good fit.
  • Microfibre: very fine fibres for soft, matt opaques.
  • Wool and cotton blends: warmer winter tights.

How tights are made

Tights are knitted on fine circular machines as two legs, which are then joined with a gusset and finished, dyed and set to shape. It is the modern descendant of the stocking frame and the Leicester and Nottingham hosiery trade. See how socks are made for a similar process.

Making hosiery last

  • Hand wash cool, or use a mesh bag on a delicate cycle.
  • Dry flat or hang, away from direct heat.
  • Put them on carefully, with smooth hands and no rings.
  • Choose a higher denier for everyday durability.

Denier vs decitex

Some packs use dtex (decitex) instead: grams per 10,000 metres. The numbers are similar but not identical, so compare like with like.

Sizing and fit

Hosiery is sized by height and weight or hip measurement. If you are between sizes, the larger usually lasts longer and is more comfortable. Look for reinforced toes and comfortable waistbands.

Frequently asked questions

Is 40 denier opaque?
Around 40 denier is usually where tights become opaque, though brands vary. Below about 30 is generally sheer to semi-sheer.
What does 15 denier mean?
15 denier tights are knitted from a fine yarn and look sheer. It is a common choice for formal and office wear.
