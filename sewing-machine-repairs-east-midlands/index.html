Sewing machine repairs across the East Midlands

Whether you are in Leicester, Loughborough, Nottingham, Derby or Northampton, here is how to get a domestic or industrial machine repaired.

Photo: Lidya Nada on Unsplash

On this page

  1. Workshop servicing in Leicester
  2. Industrial call-outs
  3. Collection and delivery
  4. Questions to ask any repairer
  5. Try these first

Workshop servicing in Leicester

PK Sewing Machines services and repairs domestic and industrial machines in its Leicester workshop at 415 St Saviours Road, with servicing listed from £35 on its website.

Industrial call-outs

For workrooms and production environments, PKSM offers on-site industrial call-outs across the East Midlands, which keeps downtime short.

Collection and delivery

If you can’t bring a machine in, PKSM says collection and delivery can be arranged, with fees depending on the machine, location and distance. Ask for a quote.

Questions to ask any repairer

  • Do you work on my make and model?
  • Is there a fixed service price, and what does it include?
  • Will you call me before doing extra work?
  • Do you stock the parts, and how long will it take?

Try these first

Many faults are fixed by rethreading and a new needle. See our troubleshooting guide. The East Midlands has deep machinery know-how thanks to its hosiery heritage.
