Sewing machines for schools and businesses

Classrooms, alteration shops and small workrooms all need machines that are robust, easy to maintain and well supported.

Photo: muhammad arief on Unsplash

On this page

  1. Schools and colleges
  2. Alteration and tailoring businesses
  3. Small production rooms
  4. Support from a specialist

Schools and colleges

  • Choose simple, robust mechanical or basic computerised machines.
  • Standardise on one model so parts, bobbins and training are simpler.
  • Look for finger guards and slow-speed settings for younger pupils.
  • Plan an annual service for the whole set, ideally in the holidays.

Alteration and tailoring businesses

Alteration shops typically need a heavy-duty or industrial lockstitch, an overlocker, and possibly a blind hem machine, plus a good steam iron and pressing table. Downtime costs money, so pick machines with local service support.

Small production rooms

Start with industrial lockstitch and overlock machines, add coverstitch for knitwear, and plan cutting and pressing space. See our industrial guide and equipment checklist.

Support from a specialist

PK Sewing Machines works with schools, tailors, alteration businesses, workrooms and manufacturers, offering sales, servicing, parts and East Midlands industrial call-outs. Leicester’s textile trade has relied on such support for generations.
