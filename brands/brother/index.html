Roots in sewing machines

Brother traces its roots to a sewing machine repair business founded in Nagoya, Japan, in 1908. The company grew into a manufacturer of sewing machines and later of printers and many other products.

Domestic machines

Brother’s domestic range runs from compact mechanical machines to computerised models with many stitches, automatic needle threaders and one-step buttonholes. They are a common first machine in UK homes.

Embroidery machines

Brother is well known for embroidery and combination sewing-and-embroidery machines. Check the maximum hoop size and the design file formats each model accepts.

Industrial machines

Brother also makes industrial sewing machines used in workrooms and factories, alongside its domestic range.

Where Brother comes from

Brother traces its roots to Nagoya, Japan, in 1908, when it began as a sewing machine repair business. Sewing machines remain one of its core products alongside printers and labelling machines. In the UK, Brother machines are widely sold through high street shops, department stores and independent sewing centres.

Which Brother machine suits which sewer

  • Beginners: lightweight mechanical or simple computerised models with an automatic needle threader and a one-step buttonhole are easy to learn on.
  • Dressmakers: computerised models with more stitches, speed control and a range of presser feet.
  • Embroidery: combination sewing and embroidery machines (including the Innov-is range) take a hoop and stitch designs from files.
  • Overlocking: Brother also makes overlockers for neat seams on knits; see our overlocker and embroidery guide.

Common Brother problems and quick fixes

Most “faults” on modern domestic machines come down to threading, the wrong needle or lint. Rethread with the presser foot up, fit a fresh needle of the right type for your fabric, and brush out the bobbin area. If the machine shows an error code, the manual lists what it means. See common sewing machine problems.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brother a good brand for beginners?
Brother is one of the most common beginner brands in the UK, with many affordable, easy-to-thread models. As with any machine, try it or read the specification before buying.
Can I use generic presser feet on a Brother?
Many Brother domestic machines take standard low-shank snap-on feet, but check your manual for the shank type before buying.
