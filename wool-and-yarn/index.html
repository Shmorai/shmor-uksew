Wool and yarn: weights, fibres and choosing well

Double knit, aran, chunky, 4-ply: the UK has its own yarn language. Here is how to choose the right wool for your project.

UK and US yarn names

UK patterns usually say 4-ply, double knit (DK), aran and chunky. US patterns say fingering, light worsted (DK), worsted (close to aran) and bulky. Always check the recommended needle size and tension (gauge) on the ball band rather than relying on the name.

Fibres

  • Wool: warm, elastic and breathable. Superwash wool can be machine washed.
  • Cotton: cool and crisp, good for summer tops and dishcloths, but less stretchy.
  • Acrylic: affordable and easy care, popular for blankets and children’s wear.
  • Blends: wool with nylon is the classic for hard-wearing socks.

From hand knitting to hosiery

Hand knitting and machine knitting share a history. The stocking frame, invented in 1589, started an industry that made the East Midlands the home of British hosiery. Read about Leicester’s hosiery heritage.

Frequently asked questions

What is double knit wool?
Double knit (DK) is a medium-weight UK yarn, usually knitted on about 4mm needles. It is the most common weight for jumpers, hats and baby clothes.
