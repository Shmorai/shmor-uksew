# UK SEW (uksew.shmor.ai)

A Shmor marketplace (shared rules in `../../CLAUDE.md`): its own brand, design and SEO. UK sewing machines, parts, wool and yarn, accessories, servicing/repairs and machine hire, a **UK-wide** brand (not Leicester-based: user, 2026-10-14) with British textile heritage as a theme and **PK Sewing Machines (www.pksm.co.uk)** embedded as its featured workshop partner. Repo: `Shmorai/shmor-uksew` (private until launch). Status: **building**, no referral links yet. Brand name is **"UK SEW"** (all caps, the user’s choice on 2026-10-14; earlier "UKsew", then "UK Sew"); id/subdomain stay `uksew`.

## Design
**v4 "British heritage outfitter"** (2026-10-14, user: "more British, tweed patterns and card styles") layered on top of v3 at the end of `assets/site.css`: oatmeal #F5F0E6, navy #14213D, burgundy #8A1C2B (accent and stitch), racing green #1F3B2D (button hover), mustard-brass #9C7633 labels. **Tweed:** four generated seamless herringbone tiles with coloured flecks as CSS variables `--tweed-navy`, `--tweed-heather`, `--tweed-green` and `--tweed-oat` (generated by a script; the data URIs are in the "Tweed textures" block). They're used on the top line, footer, brand strip and CTA band (navy), the dark feature band (green), soft bands, the facts band and the inner-page hero text side (oat), and the swatch behind the homepage collage (heather, with a pinked edge). **Tailoring details:** catalogue cards are fabric swatches (photo with pinked edge via `--pinked` mask, woven-label kicker with a dashed border, stitched patch outline on hover), deal badges are swing tags, step numbers are navy sewing buttons with a dashed ring, and feature photos have a dashed stitch inset. Corners are tighter (6px).
**Footer (compact):** brand and one line, then three short link columns (Guides, Learn, UK SEW: Deals, Workshop partner, Shmor.ai network, Contact), then a legal bar (© · Part of the Shmor.ai network · Privacy · Cookies · Terms · How we make money) and a one-sentence disclosure. It no longer uses `{{footer_nav}}`. Height is about 290px on desktop and 575px on mobile.
