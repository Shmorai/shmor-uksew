Leicester: the city that clothed the world

Framework knitting reached Leicestershire in the 1600s, and by the 1800s Leicester was famous for stockings, underwear and knitwear. Firms such as N. Corah & Sons, whose St Margaret’s Works was one of the largest hosiery factories in Britain, and Wolsey, named after Cardinal Wolsey who is buried at Leicester Abbey, made the city a byword for knitted goods.
