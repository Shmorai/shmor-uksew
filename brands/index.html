Why brand matters less than you think

Every major brand makes good and basic machines. What matters most is the model, its build quality, and whether a local dealer can service it. See our brand reviews for Bernina, Pfaff, Jack and Yamato too, and ask a specialist such as PK Sewing Machines which models they can support.
